3-mark question · Nationalism in India2 / 3

Explain any three reasons why rich peasant communities took part in the Civil Disobedience Movement.

Student's answer

1. Rich peasants like Patidars and Jats were hard hit by the trade depression and falling prices.
2. They found it impossible to pay the government's revenue demand.
3. They were angry because Gandhiji called off the movement in 1931.

Marking — point by point
✓+1 Hit by trade depression & falling prices (communities named — good).
✓+1 Could not pay the revenue demand.
✗0 Point 3 answers why they were disappointed later, not why they joined — a direction-of-question slip that costs a mark in boards every year.
✦

Marker's note: Two crisp points. Re-read the stem before your last point — the question asks why they joined.
