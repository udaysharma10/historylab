The French Revolution and the Idea of the Nation

How one revolution taught Europe that a nation belongs to its people.

4 topics 1 quiz Completed

What You'll Learn

First clear expression of nationalism came with the French Revolution (1789)
Napoleonic Code (1804): equality before law, abolished birth privileges, right to property
Sovereignty transferred from monarchy to citizens — the people constitute the nation
Napoleon's reforms spread across Europe, but hostility grew over taxation and conscription
Measures for collective identity: tricolour flag, National Assembly, uniform laws, common language
